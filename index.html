Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Secure Cloud Drive.

🔐 How is my data encrypted?

Files use browser-side AES-GCM encryption, but this prototype derives the key from a stable account ID. That ID is not secret, so do not use this version to protect sensitive files.

👤 How do I create an account?

Choose Continue with Google. Your first successful sign-in creates an account using your verified Google identity.

🔑 How do I recover account access?

Use Google's account recovery process for the Google account you signed in with.

📁 Can I upload any file type?

Yes! We support all file types — documents, images, videos, audio, code files, and more. Everything is encrypted before upload.

⚡ How fast are uploads?

Uploads are processed locally in your browser and then sent to the server. Speed depends on your internet connection and file size.

🔒 Is this really zero-knowledge?

No. The prototype derives its encryption key from your account ID, which is not secret. Do not use it to protect sensitive files.
